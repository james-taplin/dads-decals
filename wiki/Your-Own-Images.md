# Your own images

## Adding images
1. Put **PNG** files in `Derail Valley/Mods/DadsDecals/Decals/`.
2. Subfolders become **categories** in the palette (e.g. `Decals/My Logos/crest.png` shows under *My Logos*). Files directly in `Decals/` show under *General*.
3. In game, press **Reload folder** in the Place tab. No restart needed.

## Image tips
- **Use transparency.** Anything transparent in the PNG isn't drawn, so cut logos and lettering out cleanly.
- **Make plain shapes white** if you want to colour them in game. The decal's **Tint** multiplies the image, so a white image takes on the tint exactly.
- **Size:** 512 to 2048 pixels on the long side is plenty. Bigger images look sharper up close but use more memory. Anything over 2048 px logs a warning.
- **Shape:** the decal keeps your image's proportions (with *Keep image proportions* on), so crop tight around the artwork.
- **Renaming or moving** a PNG breaks decals that use it. They stop drawing until the file is back under the same name.

## Included examples
The mod ships with 51 original example images. They're MIT licensed like the mod, so you can use and modify them freely.

| Category | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Warning Stripes** | Yellow/black (both directions), red/white, black/white, white tintable, chevrons, thin edge stripe |
| **Placards** | OSHA-style DANGER, WARNING, CAUTION and NOTICE signs; KEEP CLEAR |
| **Electrical** | High-voltage triangle; 600 V DC, 1500 V DC, 3000 V DC and 25 000 V AC tags; overhead line danger; earth point; isolate before working |
| **Fuel and Fluids** | Diesel, fuel fill, water, coal, lube oil, coolant, sand, hydraulic oil; no-smoking placard |
| **Access and Service** | Access panel, no step, lift here, jacking point, battery, air reservoir, hand brake, sand fill, fire extinguisher, emergency fuel cut-off and more |
| **Logos** | DVRT-style block, speed-lines and herald logos; "Derail Valley Rail Transportation" lettering |
| **Test** | The Dad's Decals logo and a hazard stripe |

The DVRT-style logos are original drawings using the in-game company name, not the game's own artwork.

The examples are generated by `tools/make-example-decals.ps1` in the repository, so you can change their wording or colours and regenerate them.
